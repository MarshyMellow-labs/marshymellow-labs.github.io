    02 / TINY DUNGEON

    Find the green exit.

    Move Marshy one square at a time. Reach EXIT to go down a floor. Snacks are optional.

    No timer. Think it through.You move, then the blobs move.

    Blobs move every second step and can lunge into you from a neighbouring square. Keep a square between you to dodge. Each turn costs at most 1 heart; at zero, your run ends.
